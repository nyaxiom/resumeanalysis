# Independent resume–vacancy relevance data review

Reviewed on 2026-10-23 UTC. **No accessible dataset was established in this
bounded search that simultaneously supplies independently human-judged
resume–vacancy relevance, usable source permissions, compatible English/Russian
content, and auditable candidate-set/split provenance.** This is not a claim that
such datasets do not exist. No personal résumé payload was downloaded, no author
was contacted, and public availability was not treated as a reuse grant.

The new backend can therefore be evaluated mechanically and against the existing
synthetic labels, but its real-world hiring relevance and Russian transfer remain
unverified. The prospective [human protocol](../evaluation/README.md),
[annotation schema](../evaluation/rerank/grouped_annotations.schema.json) and
[unjudged template](../evaluation/rerank/annotation_template.jsonl) provide the
path to an independent evaluation without inventing labels.

The project's existing `michaelozon/candidate-matching-synthetic` pool has pinned
publisher MIT terms and reproducible original split checks, but its Qwen-generated
texts and heuristic skill-overlap targets are explicitly **synthetic diagnostics**.
Role Radar's locally described composite fit scores likewise are not independent
human relevance; its file is absent from this environment. The Russian
`Dashkins/russian-job-retrieval-dataset` reviewed in the
[skill-data report](skill_data/additional_sources.md) is synthetic with no verified
human qrels. None of those substitutes resolves the external evaluation gap.

## What must be verified before admitting a future benchmark

1. A clear dataset-specific grant from a party authorized to release both résumé
   and vacancy text, plus purpose/redistribution permissions and any required
   attribution. A source-code license or a model's license is insufficient.
2. Real-source or independently human annotation provenance, recruitment outcome
   versus relevance distinctions, assessor expertise, rubric, agreement and
   adjudication. Application/click/rejection logs have exposure and selection
   biases and cannot silently become exhaustive relevance truth.
3. Stable profile/job/source/time IDs and original content hashes. Verify split
   disjointness by connected components, normalized text and near-duplicates;
   document any deliberately shared catalog and avoid advertising that as a
   cold-job test. Keep augmentation, translations and template siblings together.
4. Genuinely grouped candidate pools with all intended pool members judged, or an
   explicit incomplete-judgment protocol. Never fill unjudged pairs with negative
   labels. Record candidate-pool construction and recall denominator coverage.
5. Compatible EN/RU task scope and privacy/consent constraints. Machine translation
   and model-generated judgments are additional interventions, not independent
   Russian human validation.

The machine-readable [evidence snapshot](rerank_data_evidence.json) retains exact
source revisions, inspected files, SHA-256 hashes and the scope of each decision.
Raw third-party text, annotations and licenses were researched locally only;
none of these dataset payloads or implementations was copied into this repository.
